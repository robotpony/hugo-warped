{{- /* One line of a line-read fence body, as a parser reads it: its note dropped and the rest trimmed (fences/README.md, "Notes"). A note starts at ← or <- with whitespace before it, or at the start of the line, and runs to the end. \← and \<- are literal arrows. A note-only line comes back empty, so callers skip it like a blank one. */ -}} {{- $line := replace (replace . `\←` "\x00l") `\<-` "\x00a" -}} {{- $line = replaceRE `(?:^|\s+)(?:←|<-).*$` "" $line -}} {{- $line = replace (replace $line "\x00l" "←") "\x00a" "<-" -}} {{- return strings.TrimSpace $line -}}
