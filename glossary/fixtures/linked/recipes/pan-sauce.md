A sauce made in the pan the meat cooked in, with gochujang for heat and umami. Kenji calls it a *mise en place* job.

## Ingredients

- Wine, to deglaze
- A spoon of kewpie

### For the finish

- Butter, for the fond

## Fond and flavour

Sear the steak, then pour off the fat. The `fond` stays behind, and [deglazing](https://example.org/deglaze) lifts it. Fondue is another thing. The [[Maillard reaction]] does the browning, and a little [[Kewpie mayonnaise|kewpie]] or [[MSG]] rounds it out.

[[Deglaze]] with wine, scrape, and reduce. Deglaze again if it sticks. Add monosodium glutamate to taste.
