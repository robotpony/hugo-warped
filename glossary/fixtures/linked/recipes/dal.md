A pot of lentils with a tadka on top, for umami.

## Sauce

- Butter and flour, for a roux
- Stock, to deglaze
  the pot after the onions

Whisk the [[Roux|roux]] until it smells of toast.

## Crust {.article}

- [[Deglaze]] the pan before the crust goes on.

## Notes

- [[Kewpie mayonnaise|Kewpie]] on top is good too.
